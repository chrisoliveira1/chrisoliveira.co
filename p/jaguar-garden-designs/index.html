01.   The Craft

Where Artistry Meets
Horticulture

Martina Paul, President of Jaguar Garden Designs, has spent more than 25 years transforming South Florida's finest estates into living works of art. Her approach blends deep horticultural knowledge with an intuitive design sensibility — the result is landscapes that feel as beautiful as they are alive.

What sets Jaguar apart in Boca Raton is a rare specialty: organic and edible garden design woven seamlessly into luxury estate landscapes. While most landscapers focus purely on aesthetics, Martina and Wolfgang Paul deliver gardens that are stunning to look at, sustainable at their core, and — when clients wish — productive enough to harvest from year-round.
