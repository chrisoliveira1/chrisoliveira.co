Our Story

Deep Roots in Palm Beach County

Professional Services Contracting was founded in 1998 in Boynton Beach, FL — built on the belief that great construction comes down to personal accountability. Every project, large or small, is managed and supervised personally by ownership. No hand-offs, no surprises.

Over the past 27 years we've completed hundreds of residential remodels, home additions, condo renovations, and commercial buildouts across Boynton Beach, Delray Beach, Boca Raton, West Palm Beach, and beyond. Our A+ BBB rating and spotless complaint history reflect a simple standard: we only close a job when the client is completely satisfied.
