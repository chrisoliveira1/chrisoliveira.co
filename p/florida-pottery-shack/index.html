Native Floridian
Ernie DeBlasi
The Maker

Where Surfing,
Art & Clay Meet

"Surfing, outdoors, and art have always gone hand in hand for me — one inspires the other."

This Pottery Shack started as an idea: create a shop in a tropical setting to display handmade works straight from the studio. For Ernie, the outdoors and the wheel have always been inseparable — the ocean shapes the glazes, the Florida light lives in every piece.

Ernie DeBlasi is a local South Florida potter and artist who cultivates inspiration from nature in the tropics. His appreciation for the outdoors and wildlife in his home state is part of his way of life — and it shows in every mug, bowl, and bottle he throws.

Thank you for stopping by. — Ernie
