What We Do

Complete Roofing
Solutions

From emergency storm repairs to full commercial re-roofing — every system, every material, every time.

Shingle Roofing

Architectural and 3-tab asphalt shingles — the most popular roofing system in South Florida. Rated for 130 mph wind resistance. Select ShingleMaster installation standard.

Metal Roofing

Standing seam and metal tile systems engineered for Florida's hurricane season. Energy-efficient, low-maintenance, and rated to last 40–70 years with our workmanship warranty included.

Tile Roofing

Concrete and clay tile installation and replacement. The preferred aesthetic for South Florida's luxury homes. We handle full re-roofing and tile-by-tile repair with mortar matching.

Flat & Commercial

TPO, modified bitumen, and built-up roofing for commercial properties. We handle inspections, new installations, coatings, and roof drainage assessments across all three counties.

Storm Damage Repair

Rapid-response emergency repairs after hurricanes, tropical storms, and severe weather. We document damage for insurance claims and restore your roof to pre-storm condition fast.

Inspections & Leak Repair

Comprehensive roof inspections with written reports. We locate active leaks, failing flashings, and deteriorating underlayment — then fix the cause, not just the symptom.
