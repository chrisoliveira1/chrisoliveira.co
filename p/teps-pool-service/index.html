What We Do

Complete Pool Care, Top to Bottom

From weekly maintenance to full resurfacing — Teps handles every aspect of your pool so you can just enjoy it.

Weekly Pool Cleaning

Same technician, same day, every week. We skim, brush, vacuum, check chemistry, and backwash — so your pool is always guest-ready.

Pool Repair & Equipment

Pump, filter, heater, salt system, timer, lighting, auto-control systems — we repair and install all major brands, usually same-day.

Chemical Balancing

Precise pH, chlorine, alkalinity, and calcium hardness testing — balanced water that's safe, clear, and easy on your pool surface.

Pool Resurfacing

Showroom-quality finishes — marcite, quartz, pebble. Complete resurfacing that restores beauty and adds 15–20 years to pool life.

Green Pool Rescue

Turned green? We diagnose, shock, algaecide treat, and vacuum — returning your pool from swamp to sparkling in 2–3 days.

Leak Detection

Losing water and not sure why? Our systematic leak detection process finds the problem — structural, plumbing, or equipment — fast.
