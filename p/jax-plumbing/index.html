What We Fix

Full-Service Plumbing, Done Right

From emergency leaks to full repipes — Jax Plumbing handles every residential and commercial plumbing need across Broward County.

Drain Cleaning & Video Inspection

Slow or backed-up drains cleared fast. We use professional drain-snaking and hydro-jetting — not store-bought chemicals that eat your pipes.

Water Heater Service & Install

Gas, electric, and tankless water heater installation and repair. Same-day replacements available — don't go without hot water.

Leak Detection & Repair

Hidden slab leaks, pipe leaks, and fixture drips found and fixed. We locate the source before we open any walls — saving you money.

Pipe Repair & Repiping

Burst pipes patched, corroded lines replaced, full repipes of older homes. We use quality copper and PEX — materials built to last South Florida's climate.

Fixture Installation & Repair

Faucets, toilets, sinks, garbage disposals, and dishwashers — installed properly the first time. Bathroom and kitchen upgrades done right.

Sewer Line Service

Main sewer line cleaning, repair, and camera inspection. Catch problems early — before a full backup turns into a major restoration job.
