What We Do

Electrical Services for
Homes & Businesses

From service panels to EV chargers — fully permitted, properly done, every time.

Panel Upgrades

200A service upgrades, main breaker replacements, and sub-panel installs. We pull permits and coordinate final inspections — the right way, every time.

Home Rewiring

Old knob-and-tube or aluminum wiring creating a safety hazard? We handle full and partial rewires for existing homes — quickly, cleanly, and up to current NEC code.

EV Charger Installation

Level 2 home charging stations installed for Tesla, Rivian, GM, Ford, and all major EV brands. We handle the 240V circuit, NEMA outlet or hardwired charger, and permit.

Outlets & Switches

GFCI upgrades, USB outlets, smart dimmer switches, and whole-room circuit additions. Fast, same-week scheduling for residential and commercial jobs across Palm Beach County.

Generator Installation

Whole-home standby generators and transfer switches — essential in South Florida hurricane season. We handle site assessment, load calculations, and city permits from start to finish.

Commercial Electrical

Offices, retail buildouts, restaurants, and light industrial. Tenant improvements, 3-phase service, lighting controls, and code compliance for Palm Beach County commercial properties.
