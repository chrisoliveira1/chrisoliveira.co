Our Story

South Florida's First
Green Plumber

"Quality craftsmanship, professionalism, and customer satisfaction — that's what Terrano Plumbing stands for, every single job."

Terrano Plumbing & Remodeling has been a family business serving Palm Beach County and Broward County since 1994. As South Florida's pioneering Licensed Green Plumber, we were founding members of the Green Plumbers USA licensing program — earning Green Plumber of the Year honors in both 2012 and 2014.

Every job — from a dripping faucet to a full bathroom remodel — is handled by our licensed, background-checked technicians. We are EPA WaterSense Partners, EPA Lead-Safe Certified, and proud recipients of Angie's List Super Service Awards from 2015 through 2020.
