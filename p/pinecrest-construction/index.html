Our Story

Since 2000, Building South Florida's Finest Homes

Pinecrest Construction was founded on a simple principle: every client deserves the same level of care and craftsmanship that you'd want on your own home. Over 25 years, we've built more than 100 custom homes and completed 120+ remodeling projects across Broward and Miami-Dade counties — from 8,500 sq ft estate homes in Southwest Ranches to precision kitchen remodels in Coral Springs.

Our founder, Steve Gonzalez, C.G.C., is a state-certified general contractor, published author on consumer construction rights, and has been featured on CNBC and CNN as a construction industry expert. When you work with Pinecrest, you work directly with someone who has decades of hands-on experience and a reputation built on referrals.
