What We Do

Electrical Services for Broward Homeowners

From panel upgrades to EV chargers — every job is permitted, inspected, and done right the first time.

Panel Upgrades
Replace outdated Federal Pacific, Zinsco, or underpowered panels. We pull the permit, handle FPL coordination, and get your home to modern code — typically 200A.
EV Charger Install
Level 2 home charger installation for Tesla, Ford, Rivian, and all EV brands. Hardwired 240V circuit with permit — adds range 5x faster than a standard outlet.
Whole-Home Rewiring
Aluminum wiring, knob-and-tube, or aging 60s-era wire? We rewire safely with copper, bring your home to current NEC code, and handle every inspection.
Outlets & Switches
GFCI protection in kitchens and baths, USB outlets, tamper-resistant receptacles, dimmer switches, and smart home upgrades — fast turnaround, no drywall damage.
Ceiling Fan Installation
New fan, brace kit, and switch — or a full new box and wiring if your ceiling isn't fan-ready. We handle the whole job cleanly, from removal to cleanup.
Generators & Surge Protection
Whole-home transfer switches, portable generator hookups, and whole-house surge protectors — essential after every Florida hurricane season. Fully permitted and inspected.
