From the Florida Keys
to Your Backyard

Keys Patio was born out of the idea that, like us, people appreciate high-quality, durable outdoor furniture that is both beautiful and designed to withstand Florida's harsh saltwater environment.

Run by Jamie and Missy Bailey out of Key Largo, every piece is built to order with your choice of 27 colors. Their son Charlie handles logistics — making this a true family operation where every customer is treated like family.

See us at Florida art and food festivals, or reach out directly — Jamie and Missy love walking customers through every detail, from color picks to delivery day.
