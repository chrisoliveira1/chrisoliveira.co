From the Florida Keys
to Your Backyard

Keys Patio is a family business run by Jamie and Missy Bailey out of Key Largo, Florida. What started as a passion for coastal living has grown into a full line of handcrafted outdoor furniture — from their signature surfboard tables to fire pits and complete patio sets.

They sell at festivals and shows across South Florida — from the Delray Affair to the Marathon Seafood Festival — where customers can see and feel the quality of recycled poly lumber in person. Every piece is built to order with your choice of 27 colors.

Their son Charlie handles logistics, making Keys Patio a true family operation where every customer is treated like family.
