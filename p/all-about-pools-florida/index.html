What We Do

Complete Pool Care,
Start to Finish

From routine weekly maintenance to full pool resurfacing — All About Pools Florida handles every aspect of your pool's health and beauty.

Weekly Maintenance

Regular skimming, brushing, vacuuming, and basket cleaning. We keep your pool safe and swim-ready every week, rain or shine.

Pool Repair

Leak detection, underwater repairs, crack sealing, and structural fixes. We diagnose problems other companies miss and fix them right the first time.

Equipment Replacement

Pumps, filters, heaters, salt chlorine generators, LED lighting, and automatic cleaners. We carry top name brands and install everything properly permitted.

Deep Pool Cleaning

Full vacuuming, tile scrubbing, waterline cleaning, and filter backwashing. Perfect for pools that have been neglected or need a seasonal refresh.

Chemical Balancing

Professional water testing, pH balancing, chlorine treatment, and chemical pump services. Perfectly balanced water is healthier for your family and your pool's surfaces.

Pool Resurfacing

Concrete resurfacing, acid washing, and patio remodeling to restore your pool's beauty. We give aging pools a like-new finish that lasts for years.

Our Story

A Family Business
Built on Trust

"Enjoy Your Pool Life!" — that's not just our tagline. It's the promise we make to every customer, every visit.

John Nacarato is a Fort Lauderdale resident of over 50 years with more than 35 years of hands-on experience in the swimming pool industry. Together with Sarah, they built All About Pools Florida LLC from the ground up as a local, family-owned business dedicated to delivering superior commercial and residential pool services across Broward County.
