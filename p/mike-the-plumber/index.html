What Mike Does Best

Full-Service Plumbing,
Done Right the First Time

From emergency calls to scheduled repairs, Mike handles residential and commercial plumbing across all of Palm Beach County — personally, every time.

Drain Cleaning

Slow drains, clogs, and backups cleared fast. Mike uses professional-grade snaking and hydro-jetting — not the workarounds that fail next month.

Water Heaters

Install, repair, or replace — gas and electric. Mike recommends the right unit for your home's needs and installs same-day in most cases.

Leak Detection & Repair

Hidden leaks waste thousands per year. Mike uses video sewer cameras and pressure testing to find the problem before tearing up walls or slabs.

Emergency Plumbing

Burst pipe? Overflowing toilet? Sewer backup? Mike's emergency line is active for Palm Beach County homeowners when it can't wait until morning.

Repiping

Corroded or polybutylene pipes? Whole-house repipes done properly — copper or PEX — with permits pulled and inspections passed. Chinese drywall re-pipes too.

Fixture Installation

Faucets, toilets, garbage disposals, showers, and more. Bring your own fixture or Mike can source it — he'll ensure it's installed correctly and up to code.
