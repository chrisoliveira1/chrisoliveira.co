From burst pipes to full repiping — if it involves water, we've seen it and we've fixed it.

Emergency Plumbing

Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Burst pipes, sewage backups, and flooding don't wait — and neither do we. Same-day response across Palm Beach and Broward counties.

Drain Cleaning & Maintenance

Hydro-jetting and camera inspections clear even the most stubborn clogs. We also offer scheduled drain maintenance programs for commercial properties.

Water Heater Service

Traditional tank and tankless water heater installation, repair, and replacement. We stock major brands and can often complete same-day installations.

Leak Detection & Repair

Electronic leak detection finds hidden slab leaks and wall leaks without unnecessary demolition. Pin-point diagnosis means faster repairs and lower bills.

Whole-Home Repiping

Polybutylene and galvanized pipe replacement using copper and PEX. Properly permitted and inspected — protecting your home's value and your family's water quality.

Fixture Installation

Kitchen and bathroom remodeling, faucet and sink replacement, toilet installation, and garbage disposal service. Supply-and-install or installation of your fixtures.

One Standard.

Mister Plumber Inc was founded in West Palm Beach in 1978 — and for nearly half a century, the same commitment to quality and fair pricing has defined every job. What started as one family's trade has grown into three generations of licensed plumbers, with over 100 combined years of experience in South Florida's homes and businesses.

Today we serve both Palm Beach and Broward counties with the same values: show up when we say we will, charge what we quoted, and leave the job cleaner than we found it.
