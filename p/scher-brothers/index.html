01. About Us

Royal Palm Beach's Go-To
Pond & Hardscape Crew

David Scher has spent over a decade transforming ordinary backyards into living, breathing outdoor retreats across Palm Beach County. He and his crew are one of the few landscaping teams in the area that specialize in fully custom koi ponds, natural waterfalls, and water garden installations — built from the ground up with craftsmanship you can see and hear.

Beyond water features, Scher Brothers handles the full scope: natural stone hardscaping, paver patios and walkways, garden design, sod installation, and irrigation. Whether you have a clear vision or just a blank backyard, David will work with you from the first conversation through the final walk-through to make sure every detail is right.
