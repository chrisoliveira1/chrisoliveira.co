About the Company

Craftsmanship Rooted
in South Florida

Custom Homes Building & Remodeling is a Pompano Beach-based luxury construction and remodeling firm serving Broward and Palm Beach Counties since 2011. Born and raised in Fort Lauderdale, our founder brings a unique understanding of South Florida's coastal architecture, climate demands, and what discerning homeowners truly want.

We operate as a design-build firm, meaning a single team manages everything — from initial concept and architectural drawings to subcontractor coordination, permitting, and final inspection. No finger-pointing between designers and builders. One team, one contract, one point of contact from concept to completion.

Our client-focused philosophy is simple: you should be proud to show your finished project to family and friends. Every decision we make is aimed at that goal.
