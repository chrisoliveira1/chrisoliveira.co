Our Story

Rooted in Love, Since 1992.

Ms. Marlene opened Dino-Might Kids in 1992 with one belief: every child deserves a warm, nurturing place to grow into exactly who they're meant to be. Three decades later, that's still the whole idea — not a chain, not a franchise, just a small preschool where every family is known by name.

We believe that if you treat a child with respect and give them the attention they deserve, they will feel loved, valued, and able to reach their full potential. — Ms. Marlene, Founder & Director

Our Creative Curriculum weaves math, art, science, language, and play into days kids actually look forward to. Children have an innate desire to learn — we just try to make it fun.
