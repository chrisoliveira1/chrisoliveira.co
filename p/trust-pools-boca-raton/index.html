Our Services

Complete Pool Care, One Company

From weekly chemical balancing to full renovations, Trust Pools handles every aspect of pool ownership so you can enjoy your backyard without the hassle.

Weekly Maintenance

Chlorine and pH testing, chemical balancing, tile cleaning, skimmer baskets, surface skimming, vacuuming, and wall brushing. Every single week.

Water Chemistry

Premium chemical service with monthly calcium, alkalinity, and stabilizer analysis. Swim-safe water guaranteed by CPO-certified technicians.

Pool Repairs

Pumps, motors, timers, heaters, valves, plumbing, skimmers, and main drains. We diagnose the problem and fix it right the first time.

Acid Wash & Resurfacing

Remove calcium deposits and stubborn stains with professional acid washing. Diamond Brite resurfacing and full pool remodeling available.

Salt Cell Systems

Installation and ongoing service for salt cell chlorine generators. A gentler, lower-maintenance alternative to traditional chlorine systems.

Commercial Pools

Full-service commercial pool maintenance and repair for HOAs, condominiums, apartment complexes, and community pools across South Florida.
