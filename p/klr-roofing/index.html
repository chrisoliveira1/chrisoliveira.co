What We Do

Roofing Services

From a single repair to a full commercial replacement — every job is handled by state-certified technicians who treat your property like their own.

Shingle Roofing

Architectural and three-tab shingle systems for Florida's climate — wind-resistant, properly permitted, and installed to manufacturer specs to protect your warranty.

Tile Roofing

Concrete and clay tile systems that are built to last decades in South Florida's sun and storm environment. We handle full replacements and precision tile repairs.

Metal Roofing

Standing seam and exposed-fastener metal roofing — the longest-lasting option for Florida homes, with superior wind uplift resistance and energy-saving reflectivity.

Roof Repair

Leak detection and targeted repairs for all roof types. We find the source — not just the symptom — and fix it properly so you don't get the same call-back twice.

Commercial Roofing

Flat, low-slope, and commercial membrane systems for retail, industrial, and multi-family properties across Palm Beach and Broward counties. Fully documented for insurance.

Roof Sentry Program

Our preventative maintenance program catches small problems before they become expensive emergencies — scheduled inspections, cleaning, and minor repairs to extend your roof's life.
