What We Do

Roofing Services Built
for South Florida

From new construction to storm damage repair — we handle every roofing challenge with licensed craftsmanship and manufacturer-backed warranties.

Shingle Roofing

Composite asphalt shingles installed with GAF and Owens Corning materials. Impact-resistant options available for South Florida storm protection and insurance discounts.

Tile Roofing

Concrete and clay tile systems that last 50+ years in Florida's climate. We properly mortar, bond, and seal every tile — no shortcuts, no callbacks.

Flat Roofing

TPO, modified bitumen, and torch-down flat roofing systems for commercial and residential properties. Properly sloped for South Florida drainage — no ponding, no leaks.

Metal Roofing

Standing seam and exposed fastener metal panels with Class A fire ratings and hurricane-rated wind uplift. Built to last decades and eligible for significant insurance savings.

Roof Repair

Storm damage, missing shingles, active leaks — we find the real source, not just the symptom. Permitted repairs backed by a written workmanship warranty.

Skylight Installation

Velux and custom skylight installs that are flashed and sealed correctly. We waterproof the curb, not just the glass — because that's where skylight leaks really start.
