01. About Us    The Craft
A Family Business

A Family That Takes
Pride in Your Property

John and Jessica Wurster founded Roots Landscape Service with a simple belief: your property deserves the same attention and care you'd give it yourself. With more than 20 combined years in the landscaping industry, they've built a reputation across Jupiter, North Palm Beach, and Palm Beach Gardens for showing up — and showing up right.

Unlike large crews where you never see the same face twice, Roots is different. When you call, you talk to John or Jessica. When the team arrives, ownership is invested in the outcome. Every lawn, every trim, every landscape project is personal to them — because your home is personal to you. That's what it means to be family-owned.
