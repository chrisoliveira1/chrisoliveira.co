What We Do

Full-Service Roofing

Whether it's a stubborn leak or a full replacement after hurricane season, Rip Tide handles every job with the same craftsmanship Doug's been delivering for over 30 years.

Tile Roofing

Expert installation and repair of barrel, flat, and S-tile systems — the most common roof type in Palm Beach County. Sealed to South Florida's wind and rain standards.

Shingle Roofing

Asphalt shingle repairs and full reroofs using impact-rated materials. A cost-effective solution with solid hurricane performance for residential properties.

Flat & Low-Slope

Modified bitumen, TPO, and EPDM systems installed and repaired right. Flat roofs demand precision — ponding water and improper drains are what we fix first.

Emergency Repair

Same-day response to active leaks, storm damage, and wind-lifted shingles. Doug calls you back, shows up, and fixes it — not a call center, not a sub.

Roof Inspections

Pre-purchase, post-storm, and annual hurricane readiness inspections with photo documentation — everything you need for an insurance claim or peace of mind.

Commercial Roofing

Property managers and commercial owners across Palm Beach and Broward trust Rip Tide for responsive repairs and full-scope commercial reroof projects with minimal disruption.
