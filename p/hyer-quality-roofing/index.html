What We Do

Residential & Commercial Roofing Services

From complete roof replacements to emergency repairs, Hyer Quality handles every roofing need in South Florida — with the same hands-on care on every job.

Roof Replacement
Full tear-off and re-roof with top-rated shingle, tile, or metal systems. We include secondary water barrier installation — required by Florida code and essential for hurricane protection.
Roof Repair
Targeted repairs for leaks, missing shingles, cracked tiles, and flashing failures. We pinpoint the exact source — no unnecessary work, no guesswork, no upsell pressure.
Tile Roofing
Cement, clay, and concrete tile installation and repair for South Florida homes. Tile roofs add lasting curb appeal while standing up to decades of intense sun and hurricane-force winds.
Metal Roofing
Standing seam and corrugated metal systems with 40–70 year lifespans. An increasingly popular upgrade in Broward and Palm Beach County for its energy efficiency and storm resilience.
Flat & Commercial Roofing
Modified bitumen, single-ply TPO/EPDM, and tapered insulation systems for commercial and low-slope roofs. Fully licensed under GC053457 for general construction projects.
Storm Damage & Waterproofing
Post-hurricane inspections, insurance claim support, and full waterproofing systems for existing tile roofs. We document damage thoroughly so your claim is backed by a licensed contractor's assessment.
