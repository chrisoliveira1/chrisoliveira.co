What We Do

Residential Electrical Services

Every job is permitted, up to code, and backed by a lifetime warranty for as long as you own your home.

Panel Upgrades

Outdated 100A panels create fire risks and fail home inspections. We upgrade to 200A service with modern breakers — properly permitted and inspected.

EV Charger Installation

Level 2 home charger installation for all EV models. Includes dedicated 240V circuit, proper breaker sizing, and full permit — ready in a single visit.

Whole-Home Rewiring

Old aluminum or knob-and-tube wiring is a liability. Full rewire to modern copper with arc-fault breakers — the safest investment a homeowner can make.

Outlets & Switches

Dead outlets, flickering switches, GFCI failures — we diagnose and fix the same day. USB outlets, smart dimmers, and outdoor-rated GFCI installs available.

Ceiling Fan Installation

In South Florida, ceiling fans are essential year-round. We install new fans, replace old ones, and add ceiling boxes where none exist — clean and code-compliant.

Generator Installation

Hurricane season is no joke. We wire standby and portable generators with proper transfer switches so your home stays powered when the grid goes down.
